Why C.O.S.M.O.

Credibility that comes from experience.

C.O.S.M.O. is a lived-experience youth prevention and intervention program built around honest conversation, accountability, consequences, rehabilitation, and practical decision-making.

Rather than simply telling youth what not to do, facilitators use their own experiences with crime, incarceration, change, and reintegration to help young people see where certain choices can lead.

The C.O.S.M.O. Model

Two phases. One goal: better choices.

Programming can begin with a larger introductory session and continue with a smaller, more intensive group.

01

Phase 1

Introduction & Engagement

A high-impact introductory session designed to open honest conversation, build credibility, and challenge youth to think differently about choices, consequences, and the realities of incarceration.

  • Personal stories and pathways into crime
  • Choices, consequences and accountability
  • Prison realities and long-term impact
  • Interactive discussion with facilitators
02

Phase 2

Six-Session Intensive Program

A smaller-group follow-up focused on deeper reflection, practical skills, victim awareness, relationships, conflict, personal change, and future planning.

  • Accountability and victim impact
  • Anger, conflict and decision-making
  • Healthy relationships and peer influence
  • Future goals and a personal life plan
Experience in the Community

Built through real delivery — not theory alone.

C.O.S.M.O. has been delivered and developed alongside youth-serving organizations and justice-sector partners in Ontario.

01

Community Youth Programs

Program cycles delivered with established youth-serving organizations, including intensive follow-up programming.

02

Youth Justice Settings

Programming designed for justice-involved youth and youth at elevated risk of deeper system involvement.

03

Institutional Participation

Approved remote participation has brought the perspective of an incarcerated facilitator directly into youth programming.

04

Education & Speaking

C.O.S.M.O.'s lived-experience approach also supports classroom, college, university, and professional learning environments.
